How to read this

Local packets start at their observed send time. Packets arriving from the far side start one estimated one-way delay earlier, so they reach the capture point at their actual timestamp.

RTT/2 assumes symmetric delay. Captured frame sizes may reflect jumbo MTU or host offload; the model rate is not measured from this PCAP.
